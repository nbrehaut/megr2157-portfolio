#About Me

My name is **Nolan Brehaut**

I am a mechanical engineering major at UNC Charlotte, I am also enrolled for a minor in Mathematics as well as a concentration in Motorsports. As an engineer, I would describe myself as one who continues to put in effort even when the tasks/classes get more and more difficult. I believe that I am becoming a good engineer, as I am truly attempting to soak up all of the information in all of my classes, rather than just taking them to pass and get my degree. Currently I am a 3rd year student who has taken engineering classes such as static equilibrium, Intro to ENGR II, and many other fundamental classes. I would describe myself as someone who is mathematically inclined and enjoys that side of engineering, but also enjoys hands on projects / hands-on part of engineering just as much. I have a multitude of personal projects that I have done such as soldering a Bluetooth speaker together, rewiring potentiometers on a guitar, and doing numerous projects on cars ranging from a simple cabin air filter install to installing a front suspension on a 1965 GTO (A arms, shocks, springs, brakes, etc.) as well as a lot of in-between projects. One thing that I would really love to improve on over time is CAD modeling. I intend on starting to learn more of it on my own by doing small projects and eventually increasing the difficulty and complexity. What really brought me to mechanical engineering are 3 main reasons. One is my love for mathematics, specifically algebra in high school. Another would be my love for cars and other machines such as robots. One last reason is because I find myself intrigued about how things go together and physically work, as well as figuring out how to fix them when something goes wrong.
